What are the forecasts worth?

Each plant commits tomorrow's hourly delivery; every missing MWh costs 3× the energy price. Regret is the profit lost compared with knowing tomorrow's output in advance (lower is better). "Scenarios" plans over many possible days drawn from the forecast ranges; "Median" plans as if the median were certain.
